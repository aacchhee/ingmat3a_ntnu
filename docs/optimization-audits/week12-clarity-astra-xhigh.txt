## Required corrections

### R1 — Wrong equation referenced in the exclusion of zero coordinates

**Location:** `uke12.md`, line 215, section 12.2.

The text says that if `x = 0`, the **second** equation gives `y = 0`. The displayed equations are

`y + 2 λ x = 0`, `x + 2 λ y = 0`.

It is the **first** equation that immediately gives `y = 0`. The second would only give `λ y = 0`, and `λ ≠ 0` has not yet been established. The candidates and subsequent calculations are correct.

**Minimal correction:** replace “den andre likningen” with “den første likningen”. For extra explicitness, the next clause may say that `y = 0` similarly makes the second equation give `x = 0`.

### R2 — The cited lower bound does not also establish the farthest-point classification

**Location:** `uke12.md`, lines 438–442, 12.3 reading-depth panel.

The panel identifies both the nearest and farthest stationary points and ends with “Den globale ulikheten ovenfor gjør det.” The displayed inequality above proves the nearest point is a global minimum, but only states a **lower** bound for `F`; it does not establish the farthest point as a global maximum. The claimed classifications themselves are correct.

**Compact correction:** replace the final sentence with the two-sided bound:

“Fra $-\sqrt5\le a\cdot z\le\sqrt5$ får vi
$\tfrac12(\sqrt5-1)^2\le F(z)\le\tfrac12(\sqrt5+1)^2$;
de to punktene oppnår hver sin grense.”

This supplies the missing reasoning with one short extension inside the optional panel.
